Polish always.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Find the story

Understand the audience, the mood and the moments that matter most before building the edit.

02 / SHAPE

Build the rhythm

Choose strong footage, establish pacing, and use music, typography and transitions with purpose.

03 / REFINE

Finish with care

Review the details, keep visuals consistent and prepare the piece for its intended platform.
